The concept-index documents in context

The methodology’s narrow VLSI/English admission policy governs that particular index. It does not exclude the master’s, philosophical, or other engineering material from this portfolio. Its progression from source to observation to interpretation is useful background, while the existing application’s many release/governance contracts are one implementation attempt within the mock-up.

The documents distinguish append-only source and derivation records, lexical measurement, observational graphs, reviewed semantic claims, and later release events. The acceptance document describes future execution. This reading did not turn its proposed criteria into observed results.

For the presentation, the useful account is the original difficulty and the distinction the work makes tangible. The full schema and procedural detail can remain supporting documentation.
