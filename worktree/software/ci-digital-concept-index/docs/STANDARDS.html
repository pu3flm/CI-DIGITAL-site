Status Boundary

This application encodes the concern separation and pinned-baseline policy from the methodology report. It does not fetch, redistribute, or independently refresh standards. Editions/statuses below are inherited from the report’s 2026-09-09 research baseline and may become stale; every actual use must register the exact accessible source/version and later changes must enter change control.

Standards by Concern

Architecture description ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010:2022 Maintain distinct concerns/views and stakeholder purposes. Does not prescribe this app’s method or make one diagram the architecture.
Terminology principles ISO 704:2022 Separate concept, definition, designation, and domain. Does not supply CI-DIGITAL VLSI meanings.

Measurement ISO/IEC/IEEE 15939:2017 Define information need, measure, valid inputs, and limitations. Counts are not importance, significance, or truth.

Native-Domain Separation

Computer Science/interface terminology, Electrical/Electronic Engineering VLSI terminology, and general systems/assurance terminology retain their own authorities and definitions. Shared words are treated as domain-specific homonyms until a scoped mapping is supported. Telecommunications, geography/policy, and construction/BIM sources are not VLSI corpora by proximity.

Recording Policy

For every standards-derived warrant, record:

  • authority and exact standard/scheme version;
  • authenticated source version and hash/status;
  • exact clause/page/segment locator;
  • scope and VLSI applicability;
  • rights/access limitation;
  • supersession/status as a new external scheme version; and
  • change/impact/reverification/revalidation records when the baseline changes.

The app never silently rebinds a claim from one standard edition to another.
